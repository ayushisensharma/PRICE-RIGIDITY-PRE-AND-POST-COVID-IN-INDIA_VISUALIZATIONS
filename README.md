# Price Rigidity in India: Visual Analysis

This project presents a visual analysis of price rigidity and economic adjustment in India, with a focus on the COVID-19 shock.

## Objective

To visually examine how GDP growth, inflation, and unemployment changed during the COVID-19 period and assess whether prices or employment adjusted more strongly.

## Data

The analysis uses annual Indian data on:

- GDP Growth Rate
- CPI Inflation
- Unemployment Rate

The data covers the period 2004–2024 and is sourced from the World Bank. 

## Visualizations

The project includes visualizations showing:

- GDP growth trends
- Inflation trends
- Unemployment trends
- Changes in GDP, inflation, and unemployment around the COVID-19 shock
- Comparison of price and labour-market adjustments

## Key Visual Findings

- The COVID-19 period shows a sharp decline in GDP growth.
- Unemployment increased during the COVID-19 shock.
- Inflation also increased rather than falling with the decline in output.
- The visual evidence suggests that the adjustment during COVID-19 differed from the typical price-rigidity pattern.
- The results highlight the unusual nature of the COVID-19 shock, which combined both demand-side and supply-side disruptions.
